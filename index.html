Yuanfan Ying

Researcher in language, cognition, and computational models

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Brain and Mind Institute and Department of Linguisics and Modern Languages. My research examines how humans acquire, understand, and produce language, and how computational models—especially large language models—represent and process linguistic information.

I am particularly interested in connecting behavioral evidence and linguistic theory with computational experiments to better understand the similarities and differences between humans and models.

What I study

Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of psycholinguistics and natural language processing. I use behavioral experiments, corpus analysis, and computational modeling to study how linguistic structure and context shape language processing.

01

Human language processing

Comprehension, production, prediction, and the cognitive mechanisms supporting language.

02

Language models

How NLP models learn syntax, semantics, and linguistic generalizations from data.

03

Human–model comparison

Testing where model behavior aligns with human data—and where it diverges.
